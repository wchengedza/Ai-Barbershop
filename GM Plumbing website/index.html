FAQ

Common questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through.

How fast can you get to a plumbing emergency?
For genuine emergencies — burst pipes, flooding, no water — we aim to have a plumber on site as quickly as possible, day or night. Call our emergency line directly for the fastest response.
Do you provide free quotes?
Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all planned work such as renovations, re-piping and geyser installations, so you know the cost before we start.
Which areas do you service?
We're based in Johannesburg and service the greater metro, including Sandton, Randburg, Roodepoort, Midrand, Fourways, Bedfordview, Kempton Park, Alberton, Centurion and Boksburg.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, all work is carried out by qualified, insured plumbers, giving you peace of mind on every job — from a small repair to a full renovation.
What causes recurring blocked drains?
Common culprits include fat and grease build-up, foreign objects, tree root intrusion and ageing pipework. We use camera inspections to find the real cause, not just clear the symptom.
Do you offer a workmanship guarantee?
Yes. If anything isn't right after we've completed a job, contact us and we'll come back to make it right at no extra charge.
