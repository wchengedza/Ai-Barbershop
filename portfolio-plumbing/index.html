Do you charge a call-out fee?
We do not charge a call-out fee for standard daytime appointments (Mon–Fri, 7am–5pm). A small call-out fee applies for after-hours emergency callouts, but this is always disclosed upfront before we dispatch a technician. The fee is then credited against the total invoice for the repair.
How quickly can you respond to an emergency?
We aim to have a qualified plumber at your door within 60 minutes anywhere in Johannesburg. For suburbs in Sandton, Midrand, Roodepoort, Soweto, and Randburg, response times are typically between 30–45 minutes. Our emergency line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays.
Are your plumbers PIRB registered?
Yes, all FlowMaster plumbers hold valid PIRB (Plumbing Industry Registration Board) registration. This is a legal requirement in South Africa for any plumber performing work that needs a certificate of compliance. We can provide proof of registration on request, and all compliance certificates are issued after relevant work.
Do you provide a guarantee on your work?
Absolutely. All repair work carries a 6-month workmanship guarantee, and new installations come with a 12-month guarantee. If any work we complete fails within the guarantee period, we will return and fix it at no charge. We stand behind the quality of everything we do.
My geyser is leaking – is it dangerous?
A leaking geyser should always be treated as urgent. Switch off the power supply to the geyser at your electrical board immediately, and turn off the cold water supply to the geyser if you can find the isolation valve. Then call us – a leaking geyser can cause significant structural water damage if left, and there is a risk of electrical hazard. We treat geyser emergencies as priority callouts.
What areas in Johannesburg do you cover?
We service the entire greater Johannesburg and Gauteng region including Sandton, Randburg, Roodepoort, Soweto, Midrand, Fourways, Braamfontein, Rosebank, Parkhurst, Kempton Park, Benoni, Alberton, Centurion, and surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, give us a call.
Do you handle insurance claims for water damage?
Yes, we work with all major South African insurers including Outsurance, Santam, Hollard, and Discovery. We can provide detailed written reports, photos, and invoices in the format required for your insurance claim. Our admin team can also liaise directly with your insurer's assessor on your behalf.
How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?
Common signs of a hidden leak include: an unexpectedly high water bill, the sound of running water when all taps are off, damp patches on walls or ceilings, mould or mildew without an obvious cause, or low water pressure. Turn off all water-using appliances and check if your meter is still running – if it is, you likely have a leak. Call us for a professional leak detection service using acoustic and thermal imaging technology.
